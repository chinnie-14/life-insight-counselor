# Example: Event-Strategy Consultation

## User Request

`I have an offer from a startup and two weeks to decide. My current job is secure but stagnant. I am excited by the startup role, but I am also afraid I am being seduced by novelty and will regret losing stability.`

## Strong Response Shape

### 1. Core question

This is not only a job-choice question. It is a question about how you relate to risk when growth and security are no longer aligned.

### 2. Scientific lens

The key issue is whether your attraction to the startup is grounded in genuine fit or is functioning as an emotional escape from stagnation. Those are not the same. A useful distinction is this: if the new role gives you more ownership, learning velocity, and better use of your strengths, the pull may be meaningful. If it only gives relief from boredom, the risk of projection is higher. Your decision should be based less on mood and more on whether the new environment matches the way you sustainably perform.

### 3. Chinese symbolic lens

Symbolically, this kind of choice often highlights the balance between security structure and expansion drive. BaZi would frame it as a question of whether the current period favors consolidation or movement. Zi Wei Dou Shu would add whether the person's role pattern grows better through responsibility in a stable structure or through influence in a changing one. The symbolic contribution here is not a guaranteed yes or no. It is a reminder to assess whether movement now is strategic or reactive.

### 4. Western symbolic lens

Astrologically, this would be read as tension between safety and growth. The useful insight is whether your ambition is seeking real expression or whether restlessness is dressing itself up as destiny. A strong growth signature can support change, but only if the practical container is real.

### 5. High-confidence integrated insights

- You should not decide from excitement alone.
- Stagnation is a real problem, but escape and alignment are different.
- The right move depends on whether the new role gives you better use of agency, not just more stimulation.

### 6. Practical guidance

`what favors action`: clear scope, strong manager, real runway, visible learning, financial buffer, role fit

`what favors waiting`: vague role, weak funding, no buffer, repeated pattern of impulse changes, burnout distortion

`what to verify before deciding`: reporting line, runway, decision power, expected hours, success metrics after 90 days

`best next move`: do one reality-based diligence pass before deciding. If the offer still looks structurally better, choose expansion with a buffer rather than staying in underused safety.

## Why This Example Works

- It turns prediction into scenario guidance.
- It stays practical and time-aware.
- It uses symbolic systems as interpretive support rather than certainty.
