# Example: Transparent Support Breakdown

## User Request

`Which parts of your conclusion are actually supported by BaZi and astrology, and which parts are mainly based on my real-life situation?`

## Strong Response Shape

### 1. What is hard support from reality

The strongest support comes from the user's actual context:

- current role mismatch
- organizational reshuffle
- desire for promotion
- repeated high-opening and low-closing pattern
- external life constraints such as fertility timing or energy limits

### 2. What is supported by Chinese symbolic systems

Present only the parts that remain stable under the chosen method or across multiple methods.

Example:

- `Both candidate BaZi interpretations still point toward a need for role solidity rather than indefinite suspension.`
- `The symbolic support is stronger for the theme of position, leverage, and resource landing than for a literal prediction that one department must win.`

### 3. What is supported by Western astrology

Use stable placements or clearly justified aspects.

Example:

- `A strong future-oriented and idea-sensitive pattern may support attraction to AI or new systems.`
- `A strong blur-versus-structure tension may support the warning against chasing a narrative without a real role.`

### 4. What remains uncertain

Say this plainly:

- `I am not treating rising sign, houses, or time-sensitive palace claims as hard evidence here.`
- `The practical judgment is stronger than the exact metaphysical explanation.`

## Why This Example Works

- It separates evidence levels instead of blending everything together.
- It makes the reading more trustworthy.
- It protects the skill from sounding more certain than it is.
