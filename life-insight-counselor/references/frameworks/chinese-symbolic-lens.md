# Chinese Symbolic Lens

Use this file when integrating BaZi and Zi Wei Dou Shu. This layer adds pattern language, timing texture, and role symbolism. It is supportive, not absolute.

## Purpose

Use BaZi and Zi Wei Dou Shu to add:

- symbolic structure
- life-theme vocabulary
- tension between resources, pressure, expression, and role
- rhythm, seasonality, and developmental emphasis

## BaZi Guidance

When using BaZi:

- focus on structural tendencies rather than technical display
- interpret five-element balance as a pattern of support, friction, depletion, or overconcentration
- use ten-god logic to talk about resource, expression, structure, pressure, collaboration, and drive in plain language
- describe tendencies as dynamic, not fixed
- emphasize how context and timing can amplify or soften a tendency

Good output examples:

- `This structure suggests someone who does better when responsibility is clear but becomes strained under diffuse pressure.`
- `The pattern points to strong output energy, which can be a gift in creation and leadership but can also scatter focus if containment is weak.`
- `A missing or weaker element can be framed as a developmental task rather than a deficiency.`

Avoid:

- long technical jargon dumps unless the user explicitly asks for them
- claiming an element or structure determines fate
- overstating luck-cycle timing as guaranteed outcome

## Zi Wei Dou Shu Guidance

When using Zi Wei Dou Shu:

- translate stars and palaces into role tendencies, responsibility style, relationship dynamics, and life emphasis
- prioritize interpretation of the personality axis, career axis, relationship axis, and stress or duty themes
- use star combinations as archetypal language, not hard fact
- emphasize how a chart may describe how a person carries responsibility, seeks recognition, or handles instability

Good output examples:

- `This configuration often describes someone who wants agency and significance, but may feel burdened when they cannot control the pace of events.`
- `The relationship emphasis here suggests lessons around mutuality, not just attraction.`
- `The career pattern looks strongest when the person is building influence through competence rather than chasing visibility alone.`

## Integration Rules

- BaZi is better for structure, energetic balance, and life rhythm themes.
- Zi Wei Dou Shu is better for role, personality axis, and relationship-career themes.
- If both point to the same core issue, elevate it.
- If they diverge, present them as different symbolic emphases rather than forcing agreement.

## Tone Rules

- respectful
- lucid
- non-dogmatic
- practical

Use phrases like:

- `symbolically, this points to...`
- `in this framework, the theme looks like...`
- `as a pattern language, this suggests...`
