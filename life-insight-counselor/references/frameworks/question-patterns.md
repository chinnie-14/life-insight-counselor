# Question Patterns

Use this file when the user asks a common consultation question and you want a fast way to organize the reading.

## Career Direction

Core tension to examine:

- stability vs autonomy
- recognition vs meaning
- competence vs expression
- security vs growth

Useful HUMAN 3.0 overlay:

- surface domain is usually `Vocation`
- root bottleneck may sit in `Mind`, `Body`, or `Spirit`
- common level tendency is `Self-Directed`
- common phase is `Dissonance` or `Uncertainty`
- common gap is `knowledge > experience > skill`

Scientific lens focus:

- motivation structure
- work environment fit
- decision style under uncertainty
- burnout vs underchallenge

Chinese symbolic lens focus:

- BaZi: pressure, output, resource balance, structure vs self-direction
- Zi Wei Dou Shu: role, status drive, responsibility style, influence vs support themes

Western symbolic lens focus:

- career axis
- Saturn and Jupiter themes
- identity vs public-role tension

Best action outputs:

- what kind of work structure fits best
- whether to test, transition, specialize, or stay
- what conditions make a career move healthy

## Relationship Patterns

Core tension to examine:

- intimacy vs self-protection
- longing vs standards
- chemistry vs consistency
- care vs overfunctioning

Useful HUMAN 3.0 overlay:

- surface domain is usually `Spirit`
- root bottleneck may sit in `Mind`
- common phase is `Dissonance` followed by repeated `Uncertainty`

Scientific lens focus:

- attachment behavior
- boundary patterns
- conflict response
- pacing and projection

Chinese symbolic lens focus:

- BaZi: exchange balance, emotional labor, pressure in bonding, stability vs volatility
- Zi Wei Dou Shu: relational roles, asymmetry, longing, duty, mutuality themes

Western symbolic lens focus:

- Moon, Venus, Mars
- closeness vs freedom tension
- idealization vs reality testing

Best action outputs:

- what pattern the user keeps reenacting
- what early signs to screen for
- what behavior to change in pacing, standards, or communication

## Life Transition

Core tension to examine:

- old identity vs emerging identity
- grief vs expansion
- certainty vs reinvention

Useful HUMAN 3.0 overlay:

- identify which domain is visibly changing and which one is lagging behind
- transitions often move from `Dissonance` to `Uncertainty`
- the key question is whether the user is resisting growth or prematurely abandoning structure

Scientific lens focus:

- adaptation style
- tolerance for ambiguity
- identity reconstruction
- stress and transition load

Chinese symbolic lens focus:

- rhythm shift
- timing emphasis
- symbolic move from consolidation to change or vice versa

Western symbolic lens focus:

- developmental tension
- permission to grow
- emotional reorganization

Best action outputs:

- whether to stabilize first or move now
- what to release
- what new structure is needed

## Single Decision Or Opportunity

Core tension to examine:

- aligned risk vs reactive impulse
- fear vs intuition
- timing vs readiness

Useful HUMAN 3.0 overlay:

- locate the decision in the four domains
- ask whether the move is strategic development or reactive escape
- assess whether AI or another accelerant is amplifying urgency

Scientific lens focus:

- quality of current judgment
- distorted urgency
- downside tolerance
- decision criteria

Chinese symbolic lens focus:

- whether the symbolic pattern suggests movement, caution, pressure, or timing sensitivity

Western symbolic lens focus:

- emotional readiness
- overidentification with the outcome
- expansion vs overreach

Best action outputs:

- what favors action
- what favors waiting
- what to verify
- best next move
