# HUMAN 3.0 Integration

Use this file when the consultation should explicitly incorporate the development logic inspired by Dan Koe's HUMAN 3.0 prompt. Keep the language practical and adapted to this skill's style rather than copying the source's marketing tone.

## Source

Primary inspiration:

- Dan Koe, [Prompt: HUMAN 3.0 - Self-Discovery and Transformation Through AI](https://letters.thedankoe.com/p/prompt-human-30-self-discovery-and)

This file does not reproduce the original prompt verbatim. It adapts selected ideas into a counseling-oriented framework that works alongside behavioral reasoning, Chinese metaphysical systems, and Western astrology.

## What To Borrow

This skill should borrow five useful ideas from HUMAN 3.0:

1. people often try to solve a visible problem while the real blockage sits in another domain
2. development should be viewed across multiple life domains rather than one isolated issue
3. each domain has a current developmental stage, not just a static trait
4. growth requires converting knowledge into experience and then into skill
5. accelerants like AI can help, but only when the user's foundation is strong enough

## Four-Domain Map

Use this four-domain map as an optional diagnostic layer before the scientific and symbolic synthesis.

### 1. Mind

Internal mental world:

- beliefs
- interpretation style
- self-concept
- emotional processing
- ability to see patterns and rethink assumptions

### 2. Body

Personal physical world:

- health
- sleep
- energy
- stress load
- physical resilience
- the real cost of the user's current life design

### 3. Spirit

Relational and meaning world:

- belonging
- loneliness
- values
- purpose
- relationship support
- whether the user's life still feels worth inhabiting

### 4. Vocation

Work and contribution world:

- career
- value creation
- leverage
- role clarity
- strategic positioning
- long-term competitiveness

## How To Use The Four Domains

When helpful, identify:

- which domain holds the user's surface complaint
- which domain likely contains the root bottleneck
- which neglected domain is quietly weakening the others

Example:

- The surface complaint may be `career stagnation` in Vocation.
- The root bottleneck may actually be `unclear self-concept under uncertainty` in Mind.
- A hidden constraint may be `body timing and fertility pressure` in Body.

This is often more useful than answering the career question in isolation.

## Development Levels

Adapt the HUMAN 3.0 level logic into more grounded language.

### Level 1: Conforming

Common signs:

- follows inherited scripts
- looks for the right answer from authority
- stays in familiar structures even when they have expired
- mistakes compliance for stability

### Level 2: Self-Directed

Common signs:

- acts on personal ambition or independence
- actively tries to shape life direction
- can become reactive, contrarian, or status-driven
- often overidentifies with personal goals or a chosen identity

### Level 3: Integrative

Common signs:

- can hold multiple truths at once
- thinks in tradeoffs and systems
- adapts identity without losing coherence
- chooses strategy instead of reacting to pressure

## Phases Within A Level

Use these phases to explain where the user is in a transition.

### Phase 1: Dissonance

- current structure no longer fits
- growing restlessness
- friction without clarity
- heightened sensitivity to what feels dead

### Phase 2: Uncertainty

- experimenting
- incomplete map
- identity and direction both in motion
- vulnerable but genuinely learning

### Phase 3: Discovery

- a workable pattern is emerging
- experiments start to produce signal
- new capability is becoming stable
- next step can be designed more deliberately

## Knowledge, Experience, Skill

Use this triad to diagnose why someone keeps `high-opening and low-closing`.

- `knowledge`: they understand the concepts
- `experience`: they have tested them in reality
- `skill`: they can produce repeatable outcomes

Many users are strong in knowledge and early positioning but weaker in repeatable skill under changing conditions. Naming that gap is often more useful than generic advice.

## Channels

Adapt `Channels` into practical language:

- periods of unusually high fascination, clarity, energy, and voluntary effort
- signs that a domain is becoming developmentally alive

Use this carefully. A channel is not proof the user should blow up their life. It is a clue about where energy and growth may want to concentrate next.

## AI As Accelerant

Borrow HUMAN 3.0's caution that AI can accelerate all domains, but it also increases risk when identity, judgment, or foundations are weak.

When the user is considering an AI role, AI-first pivot, or heavy AI reliance, assess:

- do they have enough domain grounding to use AI well
- are they building real capability or borrowed capability
- are they using AI to sharpen judgment or to avoid thinking
- does the AI path strengthen long-term leverage or just offer short-term narrative heat

Use plain warnings such as:

- `AI can accelerate you, but it can also let you borrow a sense of competence you have not yet built.`
- `The relevant question is not whether AI is hot, but whether this move helps you build durable advantage.`

## Boundaries

- Do not force every consultation into all four domains if the data is thin.
- Do not label users with archetypes unless it truly helps.
- Do not use HUMAN 3.0 jargon as a substitute for real analysis.
- If the model adds noise, keep only the parts that sharpen the reading.
