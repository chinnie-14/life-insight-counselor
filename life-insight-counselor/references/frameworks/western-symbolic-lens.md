# Western Symbolic Lens

Use this file when adding Western natal-chart interpretation. This layer is supportive and should remain lighter than the scientific layer.

## Purpose

Use the natal-chart lens to add:

- identity symbolism
- emotional-needs language
- social and relational style
- vocation and public-role themes
- growth tension and developmental lessons

## What To Prioritize

Prefer high-level interpretation of:

- sun: central identity and vitality style
- moon: emotional needs and regulation style
- rising or ascendant: presentation, instinctive style, and first-contact pattern
- venus and mars: attraction, desire, relating, and pursuit
- saturn and jupiter: discipline, fear, growth, permission, and expansion
- midheaven or career axis: public role and ambition themes

If you do not have enough birth precision for houses or rising sign, say so and reduce confidence.

## Writing Guidance

- keep the language archetypal but grounded
- use the chart to illuminate internal tensions rather than to decorate the answer
- translate planetary conflict into real-life patterns: hesitation, intensity, over-responsibility, hunger for meaning, conflict between freedom and closeness

Good output examples:

- `This chart symbolism supports a pattern where emotional security and self-expression do not always move at the same speed.`
- `The vocational axis suggests fulfillment through visible contribution, but the pressure theme warns against building identity entirely around performance.`
- `Relationally, the chart points to strong attachment needs alongside a need for personal space, which can create mixed signals if not made conscious.`

## Avoid

- exhaustive aspect listings
- romanticized destiny claims
- treating transits or progressions as certainty machines
- leaning so hard on astrology that the rest of the consultation disappears

## Integration Rules

- use Western astrology to sharpen the emotional and identity language
- let it support, not overpower, the main conclusion
- if it conflicts with the scientific layer, name it as symbolic tension
